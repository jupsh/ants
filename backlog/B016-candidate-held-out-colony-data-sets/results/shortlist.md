## What each candidate could add

### C01 — Pasquier & Grüter 2016 — colony foraging

Dataset: [10.5061/dryad.9j1cp](https://doi.org/10.5061/dryad.9j1cp); article citation: [10.1093/beheco/arw079](https://doi.org/10.1093/beheco/arw079).

**Species:** Lasius niger (repository keyword)

**Lab/provenance:** University of Lausanne; Pasquier and Grüter. Same institution as a calibration source; metadata does not establish a separate lab or independent colonies.

**Recorded variables/material:** Title identifies individual learning, exploratory activity and colony foraging success. Workbook schema, recruitment time series, contacts and food-transfer variables are unverified.

**Potential use:** Same-species colony-foraging candidate; a narrower prospective test than joint in-nest food sharing/contact validation.

**Exposure:** Outcome-bearing title only: association between individual traits and colony foraging. No effect sizes or data values inspected.

**Remaining question:** Confirm workbook variables, apparatus, colony independence and possible laboratory overlap using only separately authorized descriptor/Methods sections.

### C02 — Czaczkes & Heinze 2015 — trail deposition

Dataset: [10.5061/dryad.mr73c](https://doi.org/10.5061/dryad.mr73c); article citation: [10.1098/rspb.2015.0679](https://doi.org/10.1098/rspb.2015.0679).

**Species:** Lasius niger (repository keyword)

**Lab/provenance:** University of Regensburg, Czaczkes and Heinze; a different listed institution from the ULB/Lausanne calibration sources. Colony/data independence is not established by affiliation.

**Recorded variables/material:** Title and keywords identify pheromone deposition, changing environment, errors and memory. A raw-data workbook is catalogued; units and row schema are unknown.

**Potential use:** Same-species trail/recruitment-component candidate. Suitability for the current model requires a later scope decision; it is not an in-nest trophallaxis dataset.

**Exposure:** Outcome-bearing title only: deposition adjusts with environment/error probability. No measured direction, magnitude or data values inspected beyond that wording.

**Remaining question:** Check whether its recorded variables map to a prediction of the frozen model, without using the study to add or tune a mechanism.

### C03 — Baltiansky et al. 2021 — fluorescence and trophallaxis

Dataset: [10.5061/dryad.j9kd51cc7](https://doi.org/10.5061/dryad.j9kd51cc7); article citation: [10.1111/2041-210x.13646](https://doi.org/10.1111/2041-210x.13646).

**Species:** Unknown in the permitted structured metadata; do not infer a species from other papers by this team.

**Lab/provenance:** Weizmann Institute of Science. Greenwald and Feinerman overlap the already-read 2015 structure/parameter source X016; possible experiment/data reuse must be checked.

**Recorded variables/material:** Method title identifies dual-fluorescence imaging and trophallaxis detection. Archive names identify a multinutrient sample experiment and detection material; quantitative schema and complete colony replication are unknown.

**Potential use:** Closest food-exchange lead in this shortlist, with a sample-data/completeness caveat and unresolved species.

**Exposure:** No biological result seen in the projected metadata. The methods title is not evidence of detection accuracy. Existing X016 exposure concerns a different paper; independence remains unverified.

**Remaining question:** Establish species, schema, whether this is a complete experiment or only a method sample, and overlap with X016 before considering a role.

### C04 — Pinter-Wollman 2023 — interactions and foraging decisions

Dataset: [10.5281/zenodo.8132463](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.8132463); linked article DOI not established in permitted metadata.

**Species:** Unknown in the permitted structured metadata; not inferred from the author or file labels.

**Lab/provenance:** UCLA, Noa Pinter-Wollman; different listed institution from the ULB/Lausanne calibration sources. Data provenance still needs confirmation.

**Recorded variables/material:** Eight interaction-labelled CSVs, an R analysis script and a Word readme are catalogued. The title identifies foraging decisions; event definitions, temporal resolution and decision columns are unknown.

**Potential use:** Direct contact/foraging candidate, conditional on species, apparatus and variable definitions.

**Exposure:** Outcome-bearing title only: social interactions have differing impacts on foraging decisions. No data values or effect sizes inspected.

**Remaining question:** Confirm species, provenance and which CSV fields record contacts, timing and foraging decisions. Concept-record request resolved to version DOI 8132463; use that version.

### C05 — Pinter-Wollman 2015 — nest architecture and recruitment

Dataset: [10.5061/dryad.35rt2](https://doi.org/10.5061/dryad.35rt2); article citation: [10.1098/rsbl.2015.0695](https://doi.org/10.1098/rsbl.2015.0695).

**Species:** Veromessor andrei (repository keyword)

**Lab/provenance:** Noa Pinter-Wollman; this Dryad record supplies no affiliation. Do not transfer the 2023 affiliation to 2015 as a historical fact.

**Recorded variables/material:** One CSV named Architecture and recruitment data.csv. Title and keywords identify nest architecture, recruitment/foraging and network theory; schema and replication are unknown.

**Remaining question:** Determine whether the small table contains a usable prospective observable and whether the apparatus can be represented without model revision.

**Remaining question:** Confirm species and whether any source colonies/recordings overlap Stroeymeyt calibration or held-out material, before even authorizing file access.

## Availability, sizes and limits

File counts and byte sizes come from repository inventories, never from downloaded data. The complete paginated C06 inventory has 152 entries and sums exactly to the repository storage size. Dryad/DataCite storage totals differ for C01 and C03; the current provider inventory is used here, with the older catalogue values preserved in snapshots. C08 has no verified file inventory. A catalogue open-access label is not a tested file download.

The full Lasius title/subject query returned 188 records across four pages, including versions, taxonomy, morphology, molecular data and non-ant homonyms. Targeted searches added other-genera food/contact leads. Broader contact/author/in-nest searches were first-page discovery only; this is not an exhaustive systematic review. Repository schemas were not checked, so topic titles and filenames are identified as such rather than treated as validated observables.
