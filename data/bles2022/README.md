# Bles et al. 2022 — trophallaxis scan data (Lasius niger)

Source: Bles O, Deneubourg JL, Sueur C, Nicolis SC (2022) A data-driven
simulation of the trophallactic network and intranidal food flow
dissemination in ants. *Animals* 12:2963. Raw data: Zenodo
doi:10.5281/zenodo.6396637 (`Raw_Data.xlsx`), licence CC BY 4.0.

`trophallaxis_scans.csv` — one row per trophallactic interaction seen in a
scan: colony (1–5), minute of the 90-min recording (sucrose introduced at
minute 30), donor id, receiver id. Scans were made every minute; an exchange
was recorded when mandible-to-mandible contact lasted > 5 s; the same pair on
consecutive scans counts as one event lasting several minutes.

Set-up: queenless, broodless sub-colonies of 50 workers; one-chamber nest
56 × 41 × 2 mm under glass; 4 × 3 × 2 mm access route; foraging area
61 × 49 × 2 mm; 22 ± 3 °C, 60 ± 5 % RH; 4 days of starvation; 3 mL of 1 M
sucrose introduced at minute 30. Forager identities are not in the raw file.
