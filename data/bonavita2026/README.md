# Bonavita et al. 2026 — red/white-light exploration tracks (Lasius niger)

Source: Bonavita P, Albino M, Gautrais J, Fourcassié V, Combe M, Lacour L,
Eibner S, Jost C (2026) Discovering search behaviour in black garden ant
trajectories. *PLoS ONE* 21(7):e0327957 (PMC13419209). Data archive: Zenodo
doi:10.5281/zenodo.19203503 (record 19203504, file
`BonavitaEtal-SearchBehaviourBlackGardenAnt.zip`, md5
a651d2cebe208ded8b9f92c590f3f1a8), licence CC BY 4.0.

`redwhite.csv.gz` — the raw tracks of the paper's new experiment
(`data_set/traj_metriques-RedWhite/Plat_white` and `Plat_red`), unchanged
except for being joined into one file: light (white/red), recording date,
ant (file id f01…), colony (col1–3), t (s), x, y (mm, arena coordinates as
given) and the integer pixel coordinates. 60 white-light and 59 red-light
tracks; the same ants were tested under both lights (paired design).

Set-up (paper, methods): three queenless colonies, 20 ants each; 50 × 50 cm
circular arena, cleaned with alcohol between ants; release from a tooth pick
(fresh support for each ant), the ant walking off spontaneously; 25 °C,
50 % RH; black curtains; white light 725 ± 25 lx (LED panel) or red light
17.6 ± 0.6 lx (Lee 787 filter); Sony ZV-E10 at 25 fps, ≈ 0.24 mm/pixel;
tracking with the in-house TOSIA software; a track ends after 3 min or at
the arena wall.

**Evidence role: held-out** for the E1 walking model (docs/STATUS.md,
contamination log). Do not compute statistics on these tracks except in the
pre-registered test.
