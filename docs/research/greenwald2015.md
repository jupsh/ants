# Greenwald, Segre & Feinerman 2015 (Sci. Rep. 5:12496): extraction

Role (STATUS 2026-10-11): parameter and structure source (prior), other
genus (*Camponotus sanctus*; *C. fellah* in Fig. 3c), never a target or
test. Read in full on 2026-10-11 for step-5 priors.

Set-up: colonies of a queen and 50–100 workers (1–2 cm workers; full crop
5.6 ± 0.3 mg, N = 6), artificial nest imaged from below; fluorescent
(Rhodamine B) sucrose; crop contents from fluorescence (linear calibration),
barcode tracking from above; images every 2 s (0.5 Hz pulses).

What may serve as a prior or structure for *L. niger* (scaled, disclosed):
- **Volume vs duration is not linear** (Fig. 3b: wide scatter over 20–220 s
  bouts). Against a single constant transfer rate (`shareRate`); no rate
  value is reported in the text.
- **Forager unloading per nest visit: 49.8 % ± 4.2 % of the crop at entry**
  (N = 30 visits, first 30 min after the first forager left); later visits
  unload less. Structure prior for step 5: foragers leave before their crop
  is empty (our recruiter unloads to `giveFrac`, 10 %).
- **Mean interval between an ant's trophallaxes: 240 ± 17 s** (N = 1009).
- **Direction:** mandible posture identifies the donor in 88 % of the 90 %
  of events where it is distinct (N = 44); bidirectional flow within one
  event is rare (a few in ~1000).
- **Event sizes:** the volume distribution drops off around the 90th
  percentile; small events are far more frequent and carry about 2/3 of the
  food over 3 h. Against one bout size.
- Foragers: 13.7 ± 1.2 trips (N = 10, two colonies) over the experiment.

Not used (E6-type colony dissemination outcome, disclosed as seen): the
accumulation curve F = Fmax(1 − e^(−0.036 t)) and the network-structure
results (Fig. 5).
