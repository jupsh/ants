- **2026-10-23 ≈ 17:54 UTC — User: draft a follow-up on whether S2 and S3 jointly distinguish ant-to-ant transmission from a spatially structured driver, with a bounded search and a stopping rule; six requirements.**
  - **Request (user, after reading D114–D124):** draft the follow-up to Part C around **whether S2 (co-movement by
    distance) and S3 (onset lag by distance) jointly distinguish ant-to-ant transmission from a spatially structured
    driver**, with a bounded search and an explicit stopping rule. The design must cover:
    1. **A stronger driver comparator:** a spatial driver with delayed local effects, independent of ant activity. Eu's
       and El20's failures leave it untested, so distance-dependent lag is not to be treated as unique to transmission.
    2. **An audit of S3's interpretation:** freeze how episodes, initiators, distances, missing detections and
       nonresponding ants are handled; controls for geometry and for selecting the first observed mover. Carry forward
       the less decisive result: **S3's excess rank association under null 2 includes zero** (+0.007 [−0.001, +0.015], D124).
    3. **Joint reproduction:** synchrony magnitude and timescales, co-movement by distance and onset lag by distance
       together. Matching the 11-frame contrast alone is insufficient.
    4. **Calibration separate from assessment:** each statistic's role, parameter ranges, search budget, fresh seeds
       and uncertainty rules fixed beforehand. These data profiles are already inspected, so any success remains
       development evidence.
    5. **An evaluable comparison:** both families must supply eligible candidates before separation is assessed; an
       empty propagation range again means "comparison not evaluable". Part C's failed calibration verdict (D121) is
       kept, not relaxed retrospectively.
    6. **A useful stopping outcome:** if both families reproduce the profiles, or neither does, name the measurement or
       intervention with different predictions and the controls needed to interpret it.
  - **Wording (user):** S5 **"resolves no neighbour dependence"**, not "shows no dependence"; its uncertainty still
    allows effects. D124, F087 and STATUS already use "resolves"; the lead's chat summary did not, and is corrected.
  - **Next:** the design (D126), for approval. Nothing is built or run before it.
