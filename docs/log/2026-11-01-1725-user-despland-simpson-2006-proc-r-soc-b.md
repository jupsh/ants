  - **The lead's judgement of relevance, from the abstract (PMID 16777746; other species, read under stage 1's rule;
    exposure recorded here):** Despland & Simpson 2006, *Resource distribution mediates synchronization of physiological
    rhythms in locust groups*, *Proc R Soc B* 273:1517–1522, doi:10.1098/rspb.2006.3471. In solitarious
    *Schistocerca gregaria*, each locust's activity follows an irregular physiological oscillation in feeding
    excitation (hunger). Contact with an active neighbour raises the probability of becoming active, which shortens
    the time to feeding and so shifts the phase of the hunger oscillation; the rhythms align and activity
    synchronises. Clumped food raises contact and with it the coupling and the synchrony. This is an experimental
    test of a slow individual state entrained by local contact (H2), with a food-linked state (H4) and a spatial
    control on coupling (C3). It also states a contrast with ants' stimulation by active neighbours, the kind of
    explicit alternative P119 asks for. Transfer is the main limit (a solitary-phase insect, not a colony).
    **Relevant: taken.**
