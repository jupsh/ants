  - **User (verbatim; adopted). It answers Q30 on D240 and Q31 on D241:**
    > **Q30: commission neither comparison on these data.** Do not substitute sugar-zone visits for food state or commission new data now. Keep both hypotheses open for a later item 1(d) decision. D240 supports this practical limit, rather than ruling either mechanism out.
    >
    > **Q31: approve D241 with amendments.** My choices are:
    >
    > - **Contact window:** 10 s primary; 60 s sensitivity.
    > - **Episodes:** retain 3 ants and a 5-s gap as primary. Freeze sensitivities using 5 ants, a 10-s gap, and both together. Report all; do not select whichever gives the strongest result.
    > - **Zones:** use the forager-visit method, without brood coordinates. Describe them as high- and low-forager-visit zones unless their anatomical interpretation is independently established.
    > - **Stage 2b arms:** defer them to a separate decision and comparison design.
    >
    > Before implementation, amend these points:
    >
    > 1. **Strengthen the location control.** Keep null 1 for the stated question about initiation beyond participation. Taking a start position from another episode does not, by itself, control identity for location. Add a conditional reference that keeps actual burst-start locations and restricts candidate initiators to eligible, observed participants in that zone. Without adequate support for that reference, identity heterogeneity beyond location remains unresolved.
    >
    > 2. **Account for temporal structure in S2.** Specify how the analysis and shifts handle the known variation over the light phase. Align cross-colony comparisons by actual overlapping UTC times, not merely recording date. Apply quiet-start restrictions to event analyses; do not concatenate selected episodes into a Granger series.
    >
    > 3. **Make observation rules symmetric.** Apply the same coverage requirements to onsets and the at-risk reference population. Report unknown fractions by role, zone and context. Counting every unknown as contact is a sensitivity check; it is not necessarily conservative for a difference between two groups.
    >
    > 4. **Finish the statistical specification.** Retain repeatability as the single primary decision statistic. Freeze its aggregation across equally weighted colonies, minimum support, tied-onset handling, zone construction and reference thresholds. Report role/zone contrasts and S1–S3 descriptively, without the proposed blanket “resolved” labels from unadjusted intervals. Sensitivities cannot overturn the primary reading by selection.
    >
    > 5. **Validate the new inference.** Reproducing Test A and Test B is necessary, but does not validate the new repeatability statistic or conditional null. Add synthetic null and recovery checks, including persistent spatial preferences and missing observations, with acceptance rules fixed before those checks run.
    >
    > After those amendments are recorded, **implementation and synthetic checks may proceed**. Freeze the reader and pass the validation and reproduction checks before inspecting new empirical results. Return for a decision if those checks fail or require a substantive design change.
    >
    > **Record these answers, amend D241, then commit D239–D241, F108 and the associated plied records through the gate.** No need to wait for T060. Item 1(e)’s mechanism remains unchosen.
