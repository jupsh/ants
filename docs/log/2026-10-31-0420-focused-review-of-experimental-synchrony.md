  - **Output (one deliverable):**

    The table's rows are H1–H7 (D231) plus any mechanism the search adds. Its columns:
    1. **Evidence:**
       - experimental manipulation (what, species, colonies);

       Each with the source and the strength of the support. Classed by the study's actual design, also when only
       its abstract is read (amendment 1, point 4).
    2. **Transfer:** species traits, colony size, nest geometry and density, foragers' access, temperature and light

       doubtful or unknown, with the reason.
    3. **Constraints C1–C5:** for each, whether the mechanism as published meets it, conflicts with it or is silent,
       or **unknown** where the paper measured nothing comparable (amendment 1, point 1).
    4. **Discriminating predictions:** what this mechanism predicts that the others do not. Each prediction is classed
       as:
       - **existing data:** checkable in the development data, which would need its own design (P87);
       - **new data:** item 1(d);
       - **needs modelling:** item 1(e).

       Examples for the review to test, not claims (amendment 1, point 1: a discriminator is kept only where the
       reviewed evidence establishes the contrast):
       - periodicity (an autocorrelation peak), sharper with more ants in the nest (H3);
       - ~~a refractory dip after an ant's bout (H3, not H2)~~;
       - recurring initiator identities and onset locations (H6);
       - bursts after food inflow, and a dependence on starvation (H4);
       - time-locking to a measured physical signal~~, independent of density~~ (H1);
       - an hours-scale trend tied to the clock or the feeding schedule (H1, H7) or to time since set-up (H7).
