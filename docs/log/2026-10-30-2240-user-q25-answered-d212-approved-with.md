  - **User (pasted, verbatim; adopted):**
    > I recommend **approve with amendments**. Keep the one-minute windows, five matched controls, light phase, equal
    > colony weighting and 2,000-draw colony bootstrap.
    >
    > 1. **Strengthen the comparison with preceding activity.** Matching only mean pre-event activity can miss an
    >    activity rise already underway. Match on pre-event level and trend, and retain the planned time courses. A rise
    >    beginning before the event should be described as an ongoing association, rather than evidence of triggering.
    >
    > 2. **Avoid selecting primary events using subsequent behavior.** Use the first observed return after the
    >    specified absence, and the queen’s first moving step after two minutes of observed stillness. Put the
    >    subsequent 30-second residence and three-moving-step requirements in sensitivities. Require adequate individual
    >    tracking coverage: missing detections must not count as stillness or residence.
    >
    > 3. **Accept δ = 10% of mean pre-event activity as a pragmatic margin.** Recompute that baseline within each
    >    colony-bootstrap draw and evaluate the interval for **DiD − δ**. Describe an upper bound below δ as “an
    >    association smaller than the chosen margin,” rather than “does not trigger.” A positive association can also be
    >    smaller than δ.
    >
    > 4. **Specify matching quality and colony support before running.** Five nearest controls are insufficient if all
    >    are poor matches. Freeze an acceptable matching tolerance and a minimum number of contributing colonies; report
    >    unmatched events and exclusions. Sparse queen-event support should yield **unresolved**. Exclude the event ant
    >    from its matched-control activity calculations too.
    >
    > 5. **Keep the interpretation narrow.** Report overlapping events and the isolated-event sensitivity explicitly.
    >    These are observational associations; shared drivers and reverse causation remain possible. This analysis does
    >    not establish whether either event type explains the 10-minute D_w shortfall.
    >
    > Keep the proposed alternative windows and thresholds descriptive, and freeze these amendments before examining
    > event-aligned results.
  - **Effect:**
    - Q25 is answered. D212 stands with these amendments, frozen in its amendment 1 (the matching tolerance, the
      coverage rules and the minimum colony support, which the user asked to be specified before running) before
      anything is computed. Nothing event-aligned has been computed or seen.
    - Kept as drafted: W = 1 min, K = 5, light phase primary, colonies weighted equally, 2,000 draws; the other windows
      and thresholds descriptive.
