  - **User (pasted, adopted as given):**
    1. **Audit S2 before Stages 1–3.** Freeze the question and the nulls first. Stage 0's nulls are starting points,
       not automatically valid for S2. Specifically test whether classing pairs by their mean positions **in the same
       activity bin** creates or strengthens the distance decay.
    2. **Run the during/after-bout contact split as an observer diagnostic.** Reopening the 1999 work requires a
       demonstrated implementation or observation mismatch, not merely locating the excess contacts. If establishing
       what Mailleux could score needs the paper, request clearance then. F093 explains a structural limitation; it
       does not itself reopen the stopping rule.
    3. **Brood coordinates: development-only use.** Approve only main-experiment PreTreatment files, with an explicit
       filename allowlist frozen before extraction. Age-experiment and PostTreatment files stay unopened. Brood
       proximity is used descriptively; the weak box/age comparison is not treated as causal.
    4. **Entry and queen triggers go to the backlog.** The design must distinguish activity preceding the event from
       activity following it, with matched control times. An event-centred activity peak alone would not establish
       triggering.
    - **Priority: S2 and the contact split.** The proposed robust S3 sensitivity (D132) stays bounded and secondary.
      **Stages 1–3 are held** until the S2 audit shows what spatial pattern still needs explaining.
  - **Order of work (lead):** contact split (design → run → read); S2 audit design (for approval, nothing run); brood
    allowlist (frozen before any extraction); backlog item for triggers.
