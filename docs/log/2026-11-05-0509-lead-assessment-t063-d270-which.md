- **2026-11-05 ≈ 05:09 UTC — Lead assessment (T063; D270): which remaining synchrony explanations the available evidence can distinguish: none further by data-only analysis of the development tracks (contact histories blocked by observation, F114 and F117; clock, temperature and light confounded by design and food-state variation unknown, with no designed variation or recorded feeding times, D240; the spatial decay not tied to a mechanism, F094); the tracks still constrain (C1-C5 with F113, F115, F116); the discriminating routes left are model-side comparisons against C1-C5 (item 1(e)), new data (item 1(d)), or the omission argued and disclosed (P03).**
  - **What (D270):** the user's question after T060: which of the remaining synchrony explanations (H1-H8; D231,
    D237, `docs/research/synchrony-review.md`) the available evidence can actually tell apart. This is a reading of
    the existing record. Nothing was computed, run or opened beyond the records cited, and it makes no new claim about
    the data, so there is no new finding. "Available evidence" means the 22 Stroeymeyt fit colonies (light phase,
    development data) seen through the frozen 2-fps tracker, their permitted metadata (D240), the Part D simulations,
    and the cleared literature. The claims live in the findings cited; this entry ranks nothing and chooses no
    mechanism for item 1(e).
  - **Since D231's constraints C1-C5, the data added (each observational, light phase):**
    - no positive repeatability of initiator identity between day halves, beyond participation or location (F113);
    - no direction of predictability between the high- and low-forager-visit zones distinguished from the UTC-aligned
      cross-colony reference (F115);
    - a small rise in the high-forager-visit zone after arena entries, beyond matched controls (F116).
  - **Why no further data-only analysis of these tracks is likely to separate the explanations.** Each of the
    remaining routes is blocked by a limit already measured, not by a missing analysis:
    1. **Individual contact histories** (H2's three-interval comparison, H3's contact-associated activation, H6's
       spontaneous versus contact-preceded onsets): contact-free windows almost never meet the coverage rule (F114),
       and no inactive interval has a complete contact history under D243's rule (F117). Both trace mostly to the
       partner and focal detection the tracker allows (D256, F117: partnerBlind). This is a limit of this extraction
       and observation rule, not evidence about the mechanisms. A looser completeness rule would be a new design
       (P183), and it would trade the limit for unseen contacts.
    2. **Clock, elapsed time and the light-phase environment** (H1 as light or temperature, H7, H8): lights-on is 06
       UTC for every colony and temperature switches with it, and start offsets vary between colonies only (D240).
       F108's time-of-day pattern therefore cannot be attributed in these data (P83).
    3. **Food state** (H4's food part): food is ad libitum with no designed variation and no recorded feeding times, so any
       variation in food state is unknown, not absent; and the user declined a sugar-zone
       proxy (D240, D242).
    4. **Spatial structure** (local coupling, H2, H3 and H6, against a spatially structured common input, H1): the
       data's distance decay exceeds the joint-shift null (F095), but a spatially varying response to a shared signal
       also passes it (F094). The zone-level directional test found no direction beyond shared clock-time structure
       (F115).
    5. **Event associations** (H4's returning foragers, H5): the queen-onset rise and the entry rises are
       associations (F109, F116). The forager-entry rise was already under way before entries (F109), and frequent
       events cannot be isolated under D212's rule (F116). Neither can be made causal from these tracks.
  - **What the evidence can still do: constrain and exclude structures.** The data fix a joint target: C1-C5 (D231),
    with F113, F115 and F116 above. On the model side the Part D diagnostic separates simulated members of different
    structure on these colony set-ups (F106), within its limits (uneven false-positive rates, shared set-ups, F105). So
    a modelled candidate can be judged against the whole target and excluded if it misses, as every Part D member was
    (F103, F104, F107). That rules out specific structures, not a mechanism class, and a candidate that meets the
    target is consistent with the data, not shown to be the cause. By hypothesis:

  - **Limits:** this reads the record as it stands; another observation rule or tracker could reopen the contact routes
    (1 above) as a new design. "Not separable" means with these data and rules, never that a hypothesis is excluded.
    The model side inherits C2's failures (D205 item 2) and the capability limits (F105, F106).
  - **For the user (Q39):** what T023 does next: a bounded model-side comparison (item 1(e)), new data (item 1(d)), the
    omission argued and disclosed (P03), or a combination. **The lead's recommendation:** a bounded 1(e) design,
    brought for approval before anything runs (P87, P103). It would judge a small set of slow-process members (a slow
    per-ant state, H2; a slower or spatially structured common driver, H1) against C1-C5, judged as the Part D
    members were (F103-F107). If none meets the target within the allotted search, T023 is closed by argued omission with its failures
    disclosed (P03), and item 1(d) stays open. The recommendation is the lead's; P12 (parked structures) and P104 (the
    queen-linked addition) are untouched by it.
