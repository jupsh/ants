    >    day. Specify coverage, colony weighting and uncertainty before computing. Preserve the distinction between rows

    > 4. **Qualify direct sensing.** The implemented drivers’ timing is known by construction; the corresponding
    >    physical channel remains unspecified. A measured signal could also follow ant activity. Time-locking alone
    >    would not establish an external cause, and a null result would constrain only the channels and sensitivities
    >    measured.
    >
    > Keep the other four interventions **“unresolved (needs modelling)”**. Approve the existing-data analysis as the
    > next step; consider new simulations or experiments through a separate design.
