- **H6 null-3 support counts computed from the real episode skeletons and eligibility bits while H6's empirical outputs were sealed (D245: 0, 21 and 20 of 22 colonies), and the same counts from the fixes brief's smoke (2026-11-02 ≈ 05:58 UTC):**
  - **Who and how:** the second H6 build implementer (D245; report `logs/handoff/20261101-2249-h6-build/report.md`),
    printed by the synthetic smoke and diagnostics and read by the lead. The synthetic data sets are built on the real
    colonies' episode skeletons (participants, onset times, positions, detection, eligibility bits), so whether a
    colony meets null 3's support (at least 30 informative episodes in each half of the light phase) depends on the
    real data, not on the synthetic initiators.
  - **Seen:** null-3 support met in 0 of 22 colonies with eligibility under the full coverage rule; 21 of 22 with point
    1's three conditions only; 20 of 22 after V4's extra deletion. No per-colony count, no initiator and no H6 statistic
    (R̄, role or zone shares, S1, S2, S3) on the real assignment.
  - **Consequence:** these counts led to Q33 and the design change in D246 (D241 amendment 2: null-3 eligibility by
    point 1's conditions). The fixes brief (`logs/handoff/20261102-0550-h6-fixes/`, run:h6-fixes-20261102) reprints the
    same counts under the new rule as an acceptance check; they belong to this entry. Added by the lead on the user's
    review (2026-11-02), since D245 disclosed them only under "Seen".
  - **Role:** the Stroeymeyt 2018 fit colonies, development use for H6 (D241). The exposure is about support (how many
    colonies the null-3 reference can be read in), not the direction or size of any H6 result; the primary statistic
    and both labels remain unseen.
