Partial Spearman of the two 3-h halves' moving fractions given each ant's rest position (median dark-phase site field
E over its still steps), D043's windows and ants (none dropped): S = 1 − z_part / z_full = 0.047 [0.016, 0.080] for
nurses (22 colonies; r 0.66 → 0.64) and 0.087 [0.046, 0.143] for foragers (20; r 0.62 → 0.58), so "mostly not spatial"
by D076's rule. The activity level does vary with rest position: Spearman(R, 6-h moving fraction) −0.21 [−0.30, −0.12]
(nurses), −0.32 [−0.40, −0.24] (foragers). Shared variance only; the direction of cause is not identified.
