2,000-draw colony bootstrap; quorum agreement before the read (D220). Queen onsets "exceeds" (DiD − δ 0.0045
[0.0018, 0.0079]); forager entries "preceded by a rise: an ongoing association, not evidence of triggering (class:
exceeds: a positive association smaller than the chosen margin)"; rise labels calibrated (null rates 4 % and 2 %).
41 % of queen onsets have a forager entry within their windows; forager entries cluster (56 % within ± 1 min). Shared
drivers and reverse causation remain possible; whether either explains the 10-min D_w shortfall is not tested.
