Pages 1, 6–7, 11. 18 five-minute scans over two weeks, 265 workers: workers differ consistently in inactivity (LMM F 2.14, p < 0.0001),
as repeatable as specialised tasks; 25.1 % never seen anything but inactive. Not explained by circadian rhythm or
taking turns. Task scans, not tracked locomotion; weeks, where our persistence is measured over hours. With F057
(L. niger, Lenoir & Ataya 1983) it makes per-ant persistence a confirmation in the Stroeymeyt nests, not a new result.
