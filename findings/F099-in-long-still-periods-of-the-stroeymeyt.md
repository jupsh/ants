Bounded still runs of 60 s or more, 22 colonies x 24 h (about 30 900 nurse, 28 400 forager runs). 59-63 % of lag-1 steps are exactly zero (simulated observer 0.3 %); 5-6 % of steps above 3x noise carry 89 % of the squared step; lag-1 autocorrelation +0.26 / +0.24. 5-s blocks: 18-20 % active, 61 % active after an active block, direction cosine 0.15-0.18, so episodic, not creep and not isolated micro-steps. Front-loaded after the stop (96 % of the first 5 s active, about 10 % after 5 min). Steady-state MSD(30 s) 0.087 nurse / 0.100 forager mm^2, exponent about 1.1 (diffusive). Tag-offset model (sign-flipped angle, offset 0.4-0.7 mm) explains 12-15 % of squared block displacement vs 0.07-0.6 % shuffled.
C2 model: foragers steady-state MSD(30 s) 0.006 mm^2 (data 0.10); nurses match it (0.091) but with directed slow walking (cosine 0.84), no exact zeros, negative lag-1 autocorrelation. Caveats: tag error vs body shift not separable; the 3x-noise threshold is a convention; 4 h x 1 seed per simulated colony; core windows select runs of 480+ frames. Read-out in B004 Findings.
