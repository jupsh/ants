Pages 1–2, 6, 10. Discrete-time hazard models on trajectories of three colonies: dwell time alone gives AUC 0.64–0.72 (stops) and
0.73–0.83 (starts); neighbour features add complementary information; a reconstructed arrestant pheromone field adds
none. Hazard falls about log-linearly with time in state (p. 6), which matches the heavy-tailed within-ant rests in
F089. Different species, apparatus and tracking; transitions, not our still-run survival.
