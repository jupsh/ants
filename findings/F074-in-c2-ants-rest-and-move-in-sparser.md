D083 Part B tables (`logs/c2/diag/control.json` → `nbr.tables`), descriptive. Nurse still exposure at A = 1–2: 0.45
(model) vs 0.13 (data); nurse rest starts at A = 1: 0.34 vs 0.11; moving exposure at N = 0: 0.39 vs 0.20 (nurses), 0.39
vs 0.32 (foragers). Nurse leaving A 1 → 9+: 0.037 → 0.008 /s vs 0.047 → 0.0036. Stop hazard N 0 → 6+: nurses 0.78 → 1.19
vs 0.51 → 1.29, foragers 0.69 → 1.03 vs 0.43 → 1.15. Nurse P(still ≥ 10 s) higher in the model in every A bin. Not
causal: rest age, site occupancy or individual composition can produce the same pattern.
