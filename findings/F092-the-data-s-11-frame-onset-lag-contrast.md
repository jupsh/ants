S3 audit (D126 amendment 1, D129; D130). Raw pooled 20–40 minus < 5 mm: data 11.0, null 1 (onset times permuted within episodes) 8.8.
Excess slope: data S3₁ +0.27 [0.10, 0.45], S3₂ +0.19 [0.04, 0.36]; base +0.05 / +0.09; Eu and El20 ≈ 0. The excess is not
robust: it includes 0 for episodes of 4–15 ants. With distances taken at the initiator's frame (S3₂ −0.15 [−0.68, 0.24])
it vanishes, but not systematically (D132). The sample is the same (98 % present) and the per-episode change has median
≈ 0. The colony mean is swung by a few 4-ant episodes with near-equal distances (distance SD < 1 mm), where the slope
is near-degenerate. The primary statistic has the same heavy tails (per-episode 1–99 % range ±21 frames per 10 mm).
Not evidence that propagation is absent. The response excess sits at < 5 mm (+0.044 in share).
